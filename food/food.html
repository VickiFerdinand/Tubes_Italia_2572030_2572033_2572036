Pizza Margherita

According to popular Italian lore, the pizza was created in June 1889 by Neapolitan pizzaiolo Raffaele Esposito. Margherita relies on a perfect, creamy balance of melted cheese, bright tomato acidity, and fresh herbs.

Pizza Marinara

Pizza Marinara

Is one of the oldest, most traditional styles of Neapolitan pizza from Naples, Italy. Despite the name sounding like it contains seafood, it features absolutely no cheese and no fish. Instead, it relies on a few high-quality, plant-based pantry staples to create an intensely flavorful, naturally dairy-free dish.
