Iconic Places

Wonders of Italy

Explore Landmarks

Pilih kategori kota di bawah ini untuk melihat bangunan ikonik.

The Colosseum

Rome, Italy

A monumental ancient amphitheater and an enduring symbol of Imperial Rome's architectural engineering.

Rialto Bridge

Venice, Italy

The oldest and most iconic stone bridge crossing the magnificent Grand Canal.

Leaning Tower of Pisa

Pisa, Italy

World-famous for its nearly four-degree lean, this freestanding bell tower is a marvel of medieval architecture.

Pantheon

Rome, Italy

A former Roman temple featuring the world's largest unreinforced concrete dome.

Ponte Vecchio

Florence, Italy

A medieval stone closed-spandrel arch bridge noted for still having shops built along it.

Uffizi Gallery

Florence, Italy

A prominent art museum located adjacent to the Piazza della Signoria in the Historic Centre of Florence.

Greek Theatre

Taormina, Sicily

An ancient Greek theatre in Taormina offering stunning views of Mount Etna and the sea.

Spanish Steps

Rome, Italy

A monumental stairway of 135 steps built with French diplomat Étienne Gueffier's bequeathed funds.

Trevi Fountain

Rome, Italy

The largest Baroque fountain in the city and one of the most famous fountains in the world.

Doge's Palace

Venice, Italy

A masterpiece of Gothic architecture, the palace was the historic seat of the Venetian Republic.

Piazzale Michelangelo

Florence, Italy

A famous square offering a breathtaking panoramic view of Florence from the hilltop.

Pompeii Ruins

Campania, Italy

The ancient Roman city perfectly preserved under thick volcanic ash after the eruption of Mount Vesuvius.
